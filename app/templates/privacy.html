Privacy policy

This policy explains how Clinic CRM handles information when you use our website and clinic tools.

Who we are

Clinic CRM is provided by Quantamloop Innovation Private Limited. For privacy questions, contact us at support@quantamloop.example.

Information you provide

When your clinic uses Clinic CRM, you may enter patient names, contact details, appointment notes and other records needed to run the clinic. You are responsible for collecting patient consent as required by your local laws.

How we use information

We use account and support information to operate the service, respond to requests and improve reliability. We do not sell patient data.

Storage and security

Data is stored on systems we operate or contract with, using access controls and encryption in transit where supported. No online service can guarantee perfect security; please use strong passwords and limit who can sign in.

Retention

We keep information for as long as your clinic uses the service and as needed to meet legal, billing or support obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence when it is no longer required.

Your choices

Clinic staff can update or remove records they enter, subject to your clinic’s own policies and applicable law. Contact us if you need help with account access or data questions.

Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page.
