Common questions

How do I register a new patient?

Open Patients and choose New patient. Fill in the name, date of birth and phone, then save.

How do I book or reschedule an appointment?

Open Appointments and use Quick book. To move a visit, open the patient history and choose Reschedule.

Where can I see a patient’s history?

Open History, or choose a name in the patient list. Visits, prescriptions and documents are on that page.

How do I change my password?

Open Account, enter the current password and a new one, then choose Update password.
